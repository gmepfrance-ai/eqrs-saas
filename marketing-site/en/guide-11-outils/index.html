Overview
The 13 tools of the GMEP software suite
1
Contaminated Land — Risk assessment

EQRS Johnson & Ettinger (V7)

Human exposure to volatile organic compounds — vapor intrusion, inhalation, ingestion. Up to 2,500 substances, Monte-Carlo simulation with 10,000 iterations.

Discover the tool →
2
Contaminated Land — HRA + Ecotoxicology

EQRS V9 + ECOTOX V9

Hazard Quotient, Concentration Index and Ecotoxicological QD for the aquatic and terrestrial compartments — food-chain methodology (Barnthouse, 2003). 104 substances.

Discover the tool →
3
Contaminated Land — HRA + PFAS

EQRS V9 + ECOTOX V9 + HUMAIN Module

Cumulative human exposure to PFAS according to EFSA (2020) — four exposure pathways (plant, livestock, aquatic) and calculation of the Bioaccumulation Factor validated on field data. 47 Tier 3 substances.

Discover the tool →
4
Contaminated Land — Groundwater transport

TSN — Soil-to-Groundwater Transfer

Transport and natural attenuation from soil → groundwater → drinking-water abstraction, Domenico & Schwartz model (1987) coupled with the Hub'eau API. Residual concentration Cc(x) and dilution-attenuation factor (DAF).

Discover the tool →
5
Contaminated Land — Groundwater transport

Soil → Groundwater → Abstraction Transfer

Domenico & Schwartz model (1987) of transfer and natural attenuation of dissolved pollutants between the soil source and a drinking-water abstraction point.

Discover the tool →
6
Contaminated Land — Regulatory documentation

Conceptual Site Model

Generation of the Conceptual Site Model, supporting the Environmental Media Assessment and the Management Plan, with automatic structuring of sources / pathways / receptors.

Discover the tool →
7
Contaminated Land — 3D Visualization · New

3D_SSP

Integrated 3D modelling: overlay of the soil pollution source, groundwater plume and site structures. Excavated-soil management plan module, PDF + JSON export.

Discover the tool →
8
Contaminated Land — 3D Modelling · New

MSP — Soil Pollution Sources

3D mapping of the soil pollution source using IDW spatial interpolation (Shepard, 1968). Automatic calculation of the volume and surface area impacted per threshold.

Discover the tool →
9
Hydrogeology

Porchet Test Modelling

Calculation of the in-situ permeability coefficient K (Porchet method) and automatic comparison with the results of GEP structure sizing.

Discover the tool →
10
Hydrogeology

GEP Modelling — DLE & Water Act

Sizing of stormwater management structures and automatic generation of DLE / Water Act filings (IOTA, sections R.214-1).

Discover the tool →
11
Hydrogeology

Groundwater Drawdown

Drawdown curves for a pumped aquifer — Theis equation (1935) and extensions (Cooper & Jacob, Hantush & Jacob, Boulton). Automatic IOTA classification.

Discover the tool →
12
Hydrogeology · New

Drawdown V15.87

AI-assisted multi-layer modelling — 42 lithologies, up to 15 layers, Forchheimer/Dupuit formula, automatic generation of the Water Act Dossier.

Discover the tool →
13
Hydrogeology · New

Monitoring Piezometers

Multi-campaign monitoring of piezometric levels (v2.9c), automatic interpretation of trends and flow direction by triangulation.

Discover the tool →
