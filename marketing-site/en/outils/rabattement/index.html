FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Create your free account on the GMEP application: you get full access to the groundwater drawdown software (Theis/Dupuit-Thiem calculations, IOTA classification, PDF file) for 14 days, no credit card required. After this period, subscribe to the full version to continue.

Does the PDF generated in demo mode have regulatory value?

No. All PDFs generated in demo mode carry the watermark "DEMO — NOT VALID FOR DDT SUBMISSION". Only PDFs produced by the full version, without watermark, can be submitted to the DDT's Water and Biodiversity Department.

Is my data sent to a server?

Your project data (hydraulic parameters, coordinates, results) is linked to your account in the GMEP application. Calculation and PDF file generation run in your secure space after login.

Which calculation methods are used?

The engine follows exactly the logic of the GMEP v15.60 spreadsheet: Theis well function W(u) as a Taylor series (transient regime), Dupuit-Thiem formula (steady-state regime), hydraulic efficiency corrections η = 0.70 and f_K = 0.95. IOTA classifications follow Article R.214-1 of the French Environmental Code.

Can I customise the PDF with my logo?

PDF customisation (consultancy logo, header, footer, digital signature) is available in the full version after subscription.

How do I subscribe?

Click the "Subscribe" button above. You will be redirected to the Pricing page to complete your subscription by card payment via Stripe (secure payment). Immediate access to the full version once payment is confirmed. You automatically receive your invoice by email.
