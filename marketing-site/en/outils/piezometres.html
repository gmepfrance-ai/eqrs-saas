Features

Method

Principle of piezometric monitoring

Monitoring a site relies on a network of piezometers read at regular intervals. The tool centralizes the structure inventory, measurement surveys and the associated history.

Piezometric elevation

Piezometric elevation = reference point elevation − measured water depth. The reference point elevation is entered once in the structure's record; each depth reading is automatically converted, eliminating conversion errors in the field.

Time series and fluctuation

The complete history of each structure makes it possible to read the seasonal fluctuation of the water table and to distinguish a natural variation from a trend that requires monitoring.

Thresholds and alerts

High and low thresholds can be defined structure by structure. Any out-of-range reading is flagged as soon as it is entered and included in the survey summary.

The software does not replace the expertise of the SSP Engineer (contaminated land), nor any requests from the DREAL, the DDT(M), the project owner or the inspection body.
