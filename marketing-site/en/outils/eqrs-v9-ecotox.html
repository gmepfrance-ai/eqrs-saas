Frequently asked questions — EQRS V9 + ECOTOX V9

What is EQRS (Évaluation Quantitative des Risques Sanitaires)?

EQRS quantifies health risks related to exposure to pollutant substances present on a site (soil, water, indoor air). It compares a Hazard Quotient (QD) to an Individual Excess Risk (ERI) to assess the acceptability of risks to exposed populations, as part of IEM procedures and contaminated site and soil management.

What is the difference between HHRA and ERE?

HHRA (Human Health Risk Assessment) evaluates risks to human health. ERE (Évaluation des Risques Écologiques) evaluates risks to ecosystems and sentinel receptors (PEC/PNEC, Eco-SSL). EQRS V9 + ECOTOX V9 is the only French software combining both approaches in a single platform.

Does the software comply with French regulatory frameworks?

Yes. Calculations comply with US EPA 2004, ANSES 2018, the INERIS ERS 2021 methodology, as well as ECHA REACH for PNEC and US EPA Eco-SSL. Regulatory updates are included in the subscription.

How many substances are taken into account?

104 substances for human health risk assessment (VOC, chlorinated solvents, PAH, metals, PFAS) and 47 ecotoxicological substances (Tier 3), in Tier 1 and Tier 2.

What are the 5 advanced modules?

The 5 exclusive advanced modules are: Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis, reverse calculation (admissible source concentration), 70-year temporal calculation, food chain, and target-organ mixture.

Can the PDF report be used for an administrative file?

Yes. The software generates a 14-page PDF report including calculation notes, VTR, and QD/ERI detail, ready to include in an IEM file or a contaminated site and soil management file intended for the administration (DDT, DREAL, ARS).
