3D_SSP — Soil · Groundwater · Structures in one 3D scene

Interactive 3D modeling software that overlays, in a single scene, the soil pollution source, the groundwater pollution plume and the site's structures (buildings, roads, parking lots, green spaces). Automatic data import from TSN and GMEP MSP, integrated Management Plan module for excavated soil, and a ready-to-send PDF export.

Features

A single 3D scene to visualize every issue on the site

Interactive 3D overlay

Soil pollution source, groundwater pollution plume and site structures (buildings, roads, parking lots, green spaces) brought together in a single navigable 3D scene, with a north marker and free camera.

Automatic multi-tool import

Automatic retrieval of data from TSN and GMEP MSP in JSON format, with automatic format detection and an integrated data editor for manual adjustment.

Integrated Management Plan module

Classification of excavated soil (waste codes 17 05 04 non-hazardous / 17 05 03* hazardous) with an estimate of the associated management costs.

Overlap analysis & alerts

Automatic detection of polygonal overlap zones between structures and the pollution plume, with visual alerts directly in the 3D scene.

PDF export & JSON save

Ready-to-send PDF report of the soil / groundwater / structures overlay, and a reusable JSON project file to resume or update the 3D model later.

Navigable camera

Free rotation, zoom and movement in the 3D scene to explore every level (soil, groundwater, structures) and present the site from every angle.

Target audience

A visualization tool for all stakeholders on the site

Owners, operators and developers

Clear visualization of pollution issues for sites subject to Contaminated Land Public Easements (SSP) or classified as ICPE, and for development projects.

Local authority technical departments

Support for understanding and reviewing files submitted by technical departments examining pollution cases or projects on potentially contaminated sites.

Third-party verification bodies

Support for independent verification and control of soil and groundwater pollution data presented as part of a project.

Reference frameworks

A module aligned with current reference texts

ReferenceSubject
Arrêté of 12/12/2014 Conditions for accepting inert waste at inert waste storage facilities (ISDI) — criteria taken into account in the Management Plan module.
Regulation (EU) 2017/997 Amendment to Annex III of Directive 2008/98/EC — hazard property HP14 "Ecotoxic," integrated into the classification of excavated soil.
National methodological guide (2020) National methodological guide for the off-site reuse of excavated soil from potentially contaminated sites.
NF EN ISO 10381-5 Soil quality standard providing guidelines for the investigation of urban and industrial sites with regard to soil pollution.

3D_SSP is a visualization and decision-support tool; it does not replace the engineer's expertise or the requirements of the competent reviewing authorities.
