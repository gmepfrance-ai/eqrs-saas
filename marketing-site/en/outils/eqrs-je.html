New in v2 — INERIS / US-EPA compliance

Σ QD and Σ ERI by target organ

Cumulative effects are only summed between substances acting on the same organ — methodology per INERIS DRC-09-103096-09387C and US-EPA RAGS Part A §8.2 / §8.3.

13 target organs — threshold effects

Blood, nervous system, liver, kidneys, respiratory system, thyroid, cardiovascular system, immunity, skin, development… each with its own adult/child Σ QD.

8 target cancers — non-threshold effects

Leukemia, hepatocellular carcinoma, bronchial cancer, skin, kidney, digestive tract, upper aerodigestive tract, thyroid — Σ ERI by cancer type.

Automatic executive summary

Worst adult/child Σ QD and Σ ERI at the top of the report, color-coded impact level (acceptable / caution / unacceptable), integrated health descriptions.

Dynamic conceptual site model

BRGM/INERIS style — geological cross-section, a-h arrows color-coded per worst Σ by organ on each exposure pathway (8 pathways).

Complete 1-click 14-page PDF report

Cover page, executive summary, conceptual site model, Σ-by-organ tables, calculation notes (DJE / QD / ERI), complete threshold and non-threshold VTR tables (~100 substances), QD/ERI detail by pathway and target, adaptive conclusion — ready to submit to DDT / ARS.

Biological consistency

Never again an implausible sum (benzene+PCE…): only health effects of the same nature are cumulated.

Threshold effects — Σ QD by organ

  • Blood / hematopoiesis — anemia, leukopenia (benzene, zinc)
  • Central nervous system — cognitive disorders (toluene, lead, Hg)
  • Liver — chronic hepatitis, fibrosis (PCE, TCE, PAH)
  • Kidneys — nephropathy (cadmium, mercury)
  • Respiratory system — fibrosis, irritation (naphthalene, nickel)
  • Immune system — immunosuppression (PCB)
  • Thyroid system — hypothyroidism (cyanides)
  • Cardiovascular — hypertension (atrazine)
  • Gastrointestinal tract — mucosal lesions (Cr VI, copper)
  • Skin — hyperkeratosis (arsenic)
  • Endocrine / metabolic — selenosis (antimony, selenium)
  • Development / reproduction — teratogenic effects
  • Multiple systemic effects — dioxins, PCB-TEQ

Non-threshold effects — Σ ERI by cancer

  • Blood — leukemia (benzene)
  • Liver — hepatocellular carcinoma, angiosarcoma (PCE, chloroform, VC)
  • Lung — bronchial carcinoma (naphthalene, cadmium, nickel)
  • Skin — skin carcinoma, melanoma (B(a)P, arsenic)
  • Kidney — renal carcinoma (lead, TCE)
  • Digestive tract / multi-organ — Cr VI, carcinogenic PAH
  • Upper aerodigestive tract — nasopharyngeal cancer (formaldehyde)
  • Thyroid — acrylamide

Thresholds: QD < 0.2 acceptable • 0.2 ≤ QD < 1 caution • QD ≥ 1 unacceptable — ERI < 10⁻⁶ negligible • 10⁻⁶ ≤ ERI < 10⁻⁵ acceptable • ERI ≥ 10⁻⁵ unacceptable.

Regulatory compliance

Regulatory framework

References applied

  • INERIS DRC-09-103096-09387C — cumulation of QD by target organ and ERI by carcinogenic effect (national methodology).
  • US-EPA RAGS Part A §8.2 / §8.3 — Risk Assessment Guidance for Superfund: summation by organ / by cancer type.
  • EPA 2004 — User's Guide for Evaluating Subsurface Vapor Intrusion Into Buildings (US EPA, 2004).
  • ANSES 2018 — Toxicological reference values (VTR) and IEM methodology (Interprétation de l'État des Milieux).
  • National contaminated-site methodology — MEEM/MTES 2017 (contaminated site management, management plan).
  • Johnson & Ettinger model — analytical equation for vapor intrusion (soil and groundwater).
  • VLEP / VLCT — occupational exposure limit values for workplace use.
