Features

The human dietary pathway, at last integrated

Full food chain

Soil → plant → animal → human transfer: leafy vegetables, root vegetables, eggs, milk, meat, fish. Bioconcentration and BCF/BTF transfer factors by substance.

PFAS, PCB, metals

Priority persistent pollutants: PFAS (PFOA/PFOS), dioxin-like PCB, lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic — with EFSA 2020 TRVs (TWI) and toxic sums (TEQ).

DJE, QD & ERI by pathway

Daily exposure dose via the dietary pathway, hazard quotient and individual excess risk — accumulated by target organ per INERIS DRC-09-103096.

Monte Carlo & 70-year simulation

Uncertainty propagation across transfer and exposure parameters. Chronic exposure simulation over a 70-year lifetime.

Full 1-click PDF report

Technical file ready to submit to DDT / ARS: input data, transfer factors, DJE by pathway, QD/ERI by organ, calculation notes and adaptive conclusion.

Regulatory compliance

Regulatory framework

References applied

  • INERIS DRC-09-103096-09387C — accumulation of QD by target organ and ERI by carcinogenic effect (national methodology).
  • EFSA 2020 — Toxicological reference values (TRV/TWI) for PFAS (sum of the 4 PFAS: PFOA, PFOS, PFNA, PFHxS).
  • US-EPA RAGS Part A §8.2 / §8.3 — summation by organ / by cancer type.
  • ANSES 2018 — TRVs and IEM methodology (Interpretation of Environmental Media Status).
  • National contaminated sites methodology — MEEM/MTES 2017 (management plan, contaminated site management).
  • EU Regulation 2023/915 — regulatory maximum levels (PFAS, metals) in foodstuffs, for comparison.
